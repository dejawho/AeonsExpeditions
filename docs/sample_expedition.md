# The Outpost Under Fire
*The Outpost Under Fire — The Great Calamity — 3 players, 5 battles*

**Share code:** `AEX-06T83-MG5ZZ-ZZZZR-30000-058`

**The road ahead:** **1.** Umbra Titan → **2.** *A:* Horde-Crone | *B:* Rust Superion → **3.** The Endless Decay → **4.** *A:* The Burning Kor | *B:* Absorbing Wraith → **5.** Blight Lord

The watchtowers have stopped answering. Refugees fill the road, and every one of them tells the same story. Raven sends you out at once: the outpost cannot be allowed to fall.
Every village you pass is empty, its doors left open as if the people meant to come back. Raven has chosen the expedition carefully: Nook, Orb Caller; Razra, the Tamer; Leisan, the Huntress and Taqren, Severed Shield. You leave before the bells can ring again.
Yan Magda's cadre wandered the spiral of worlds and arrived out of time. That is the only explanation for how Nook, Razra, Leisan and Taqren came to fight beside you.

## Battle 1: The Glass Archive of Karek

For the first leg of the journey, the worst enemy is the weather. Ash drifts like snow over the abandoned roads.
Finally, the glass archive of Karek comes into view. The great archive is a mountain of green glass now, and the books inside it are trapped like flies in amber.
It does not take long to find the signs: first the ground shakes before it strikes, then cryptids revering it. The floor trembles; nobody knows where to hide.
You form up, back to back, and wait.

### Umbra Titan

The waiting ends all at once: the Umbra Titan is upon you. Here is the burrowing beast the signs warned you of. It has no business in this age, and yet here it is.
Indira described it once: “It is a strange thing to say we have never seen The Nameless die, but we have indeed witnessed one being born. The Umbra Titan speared from the feigned carcass of the Thrice-Dead Prophet like some foul offspring. At first we thought it merely some grotesque machination, another minion perhaps, but in time it has proven to be an adversary of equal measure.”
Someone once wrote of these creatures: “I roared as it bore through the rock. And Gravehold shuddered like those within its walls.” Dai has come with you this far. So has the Cultist. Begin.

> **Friend / foe:** Dai, the Youth / The Cultist  
> **Nemesis deck (random basics from your collection):** T1: 3 nemesis + 5 basic + 0 upgraded basic · T2: 3 nemesis + 6 basic + 0 upgraded basic · T3: 3 nemesis + 7 basic + 0 upgraded basic  
> **Rewards:** level 1 treasures: Rhia's Planar Pocket, Thraxir's Shattered Quartz, Shoshana's Bottled Ink, Z'hana's Fleeting Ember, Lucien's Sun Shard; player cards: Unrefined Blaststone, Conclave Scroll, Bestowing Light

**If you win.**

The last spell lands. The Umbra Titan sinks to the ground and does not rise again. A coal fragment of its shadow.
You recover what the fallen left behind: Rhia's Planar Pocket, Thraxir's Shattered Quartz and Shoshana's Bottled Ink.
Much later, someone writes down the end of the old account: “Even the indigenous beasts of the cave seem to worship it. Beneath the skin of the cave it roars, burrowing through rock and darkness like a blind knife through muscle. The ground shakes as a precursor to its attack. It is a horrible thing to see people not know where to seek shelter, instead inert as they wait for the monstrous thing to emerge - if it emerges at all. Of all The Nameless, the Titan is the most feral.”

The scouts return with two reports, and they do not agree. Both trails lead back to the same road in the end.
- **Path A** — A refugee camp where elders rub blistered feet. Old things are waking in the deep that way, and they remember this land as theirs.
- **Path B** — A farmstead with its doors left open. A glyph ritual went wrong that way, and something came of it.

**If you lose.**

You retreat into the dark, carrying your wounded. The Umbra Titan does not follow. It does not need to: it knows you will come back.
You bind your wounds and sharpen your focus, and wait for your strength to return. It is still out there, and it knows your faces now.

## Battle 2 — Path A: A Refugee Camp where Elders Rub Blistered Feet

The trail climbs, then falls, then climbs again. Nobody complains. The sky has no stars any more. The elders say it used to.
By nightfall you come to a refugee camp where elders rub blistered feet. Tents of patched canvas huddle around a dying fire. The elders say the world used to be much smaller, back when there were trains.
The scouts' reports were accurate: night raids from the depths without a sound, and an underworld totem. Gravehold's first builders want their home back — and they may have the right.
Your gems grow warm in your hands. Something is waiting.

### Horde-Crone

Out of the breach steps the Horde-Crone, exactly where the signs said it would be. There is no doubt left: this is the awakener of the ancients. By every chronicle, it belongs to a different time.
The archives speak of it: “In the ancient time, before our kind, the troggs walked The World That Was. From what the elders remember of the lost tales, there were three great tribes, each with a purpose. The Yud were keepers of life, gifted in the art of creation, molders of flesh.”
Somewhere it is written: “As long as they bleed...” Sahala stands with you, and the Traitor stands against you. Everyone you left behind is counting on you.

> **Friend / foe:** Sahala, the Apprentice / The Traitor  
> **Treasures:** level 1: each player  
> **Nemesis deck (random basics from your collection):** T1: 3 nemesis + 4 basic + 1 upgraded basic · T2: 3 nemesis + 3 basic + 3 upgraded basic · T3: 3 nemesis + 4 basic + 3 upgraded basic  
> **Rewards:** level 2 treasures: Sky's Bounty, Bloodjelly, Thorned Whip; player cards: Pulsyrium Globe, Fiend Catcher, Draining Touch

**If you win.**

The Horde-Crone falls, and together you force its breach closed. The sleeping deity of the troggs remains somewhere below.
Hidden among the remains are old relics of the city: Sky's Bounty, Bloodjelly and Thorned Whip.
The old account goes on: “The Orp were beings of shadow, masters of ruination and death, the counterweights on the scales of life, against which the Yud were balanced. And the Zom were neither light nor dark, but instead were dreamers with great minds. The world was unforgiving and empty then, but malleable, a great marble to be chiseled and shaped as they saw fit. And though the tribes were each different, they lived in great harmony.”
The scouts bring news that does not let you rest for long: predator and prey hunting together.

**If you lose.**

You fall back in disorder. When you finally stop running, the Horde-Crone is nowhere to be seen — but it has not gone far.
Nobody speaks much while you recover. There is nothing to say that everyone does not already know. You have learned its rhythm. Next time, you will not be caught off guard.

## Battle 2 — Path B: A Farmstead with Its Doors Left Open

The trail climbs, then falls, then climbs again. Nobody complains. The sky has no stars any more. The elders say it used to.
By nightfall you come to a farmstead with its doors left open. Bread on the table, a chair pushed back. Whoever lived here left in a hurry, or did not leave at all.
The scouts' reports were accurate: glyphs gone wrong, and the burnt-air smell of an opening breach. A harmless glyph ritual created a monster — and everyone blames someone else.
Your gems grow warm in your hands. Something is waiting.

### Rust Superion

Out of the breach steps the Rust Superion, exactly where the signs said it would be. There is no doubt left: this is the consequence. By every chronicle, it belongs to a different time.
Ilya once wrote of it: “It is a common practice to use small amounts of aether drawn in particular glyphs to control the patterns of movement of weak or small Void creatures. It's how we subtly influence the Portalborn that roam these forests away from barging right into our villages.”
Lash stands with you, and Creeping Dark stands against you. Everyone you left behind is counting on you.

> **Friend / foe:** Lash, the Dreamer / Creeping Dark  
> **Treasures:** level 1: each player  
> **Nemesis deck (random basics from your collection):** T1: 3 nemesis + 4 basic + 1 upgraded basic · T2: 3 nemesis + 3 basic + 3 upgraded basic · T3: 3 nemesis + 4 basic + 3 upgraded basic  
> **Rewards:** level 2 treasures: Clockwork Spindle, Calcified Electinium, Eye of the Maelstrom; player cards: Shimmerbead, Rebound Spike, Nether Conduit

**If you win.**

The Rust Superion falls, and together you force its breach closed. Blame between Azer and New Gravehold.
Hidden among the remains are old relics of the city: Clockwork Spindle, Calcified Electinium and Eye of the Maelstrom.
The old account goes on: “I suppose with larger amounts of energy, larger glyphs could be created and used to direct the movements of a certain Portalborn, the way Z'hana did with Necroswarm, but that is not how we utilize our magic. Our practices are harmless. They've always been harmless. Whatever mixing my aether with Void essence turned this creature into, it wasn't my intention and it isn't something I've ever seen before.”
The scouts bring news that does not let you rest for long: predator and prey hunting together.

**If you lose.**

You fall back in disorder. When you finally stop running, the Rust Superion is nowhere to be seen — but it has not gone far.
Nobody speaks much while you recover. There is nothing to say that everyone does not already know. You have learned its rhythm. Next time, you will not be caught off guard.

## Battle 3: A Bridge Choked with Abandoned Carts

You travel light now, leaving behind everything you cannot fight with. There are no more cities to run to. There is only the next ridge, and the one after that.
At last you reach a bridge choked with abandoned carts. The carts were left where they stood, horses and all. Nothing on the far bank moves.
There is no mistaking it now: predator and prey hunting together, and the burnt-air smell of an opening breach. Even the phoenixes are afraid.
Somewhere close, the air begins to burn.

### The Endless Decay

The breach tears open with a sound like ripping silk, and the Endless Decay comes through. So this is the corruption that slipped through. Somehow it has crossed the ages to reach you.
You remember the account of Kiri Maesan: “I've always wondered. If all humans eventually become overwhelmed by the corruption and become Nameless, what happens to animals? They are living creatures in the same way we are, and yet we don't find ourselves overwhelmed by thousands of feral, monstrous mice, ants or squirrels. Now I know, the phoenixes take care of them.”
Qu fights at your side. Somewhere beyond the light, the Corrosion is waiting for its moment. Your breaches flare open.

> **Friend / foe:** Qu, the Rift Walker / The Corrosion  
> **Treasures:** level 1: each player; level 2: one for the group  
> **Nemesis deck (random basics from your collection):** T1: 3 nemesis + 3 basic + 2 upgraded basic · T2: 3 nemesis + 2 basic + 4 upgraded basic · T3: 3 nemesis + 2 basic + 5 upgraded basic  
> **Rewards:** level 3 treasures: Soul Engine, Memory Coin, Purified Bangle, Arcane Timepiece, Dimensional Purse; player cards: Quickening Qitite, Neural Wreath, Harbinger Descent

**If you win.**

Slowly, impossibly, the Endless Decay gives way, and the breach collapses behind it. The circle of life restored.
From its remains, masterwork equipment can be forged: Soul Engine, Memory Coin and Purified Bangle.
The old account ends with words that feel different now: “Out here, even the corruption is part of the natural way. When an animal becomes corrupted it becomes a target, sometimes a food source, sometimes simply dispatched. Even its own kind no longer regard it as friendly. I've watched predator and prey work together to take down a corrupted animal that would have been a threat to them both. And when something slips through the cracks?”

The trail splits before you. Either way, the roads meet again beyond.
- **Path A** — The road out of Erythiza. The forest is burning that way, and the fire walks like a man.
- **Path B** — A market turned slaughterhouse. The trail that way is frozen, and the cold follows you.

**If you lose.**

Someone shouts the retreat, and for once nobody argues. Behind you, the breach pulses like a heartbeat.
You lick your wounds at a bridge choked with abandoned carts. The Endless Decay grows bolder with every hour you spend recovering.

## Battle 4 — Path A: The Road Out of Erythiza

You march through a cold drizzle that does not stop for a day and a night.
The path ends at the road out of Erythiza. The road is lined with what people dropped as they ran: books, shoes, a child's toy. Behind you, the horizon is the wrong colour.
Something has passed this way. The signs are clear: the forest burning around a walking man; the burnt-air smell of an opening breach. Azer asks for help: a burning man is walking toward the village.
The bells are far away. There is no one to call for help.

### The Burning Kor

The ground shudders. Out of the dark, the Burning Kor rises to meet you. Before you stands the living inferno itself. It should not be here at all: it belongs to another age.
Kel left a record of it: “Ever since New Gravehold appeared on the surface, Azer has known nothing but trouble. We live by strict rules, our sigils minimise the number of breaches we need to have open, and we use little to no magic in order to not attract the attention of dangerous roving Portalborn. But that city does not understand how to live as we do.”
You are not alone: Grimoire is with you. But neither is the enemy — the Mask has followed you here. Fight.

> **Friend / foe:** Grimoire, the Archive / The Mask  
> **Treasures:** level 1: each player; level 2: one for the group; level 3: each player  
> **Nemesis deck (random basics from your collection):** T1: 3 nemesis + 2 basic + 3 upgraded basic · T2: 3 nemesis + 1 basic + 5 upgraded basic · T3: 3 nemesis + 0 basic + 7 upgraded basic  
> **Rewards:** level 4 treasures: Celestial Slayer, Phantasmal Echo, Pyroclastic Slag, Fiend Neutralizer, Regenerating Scythe; mage: Indira (Beyond the Breach); player cards: Crystal, Polyphase Turbine, Drown in Flames, Triplite Core, Defensive Dome, Convection Field

**If you win.**

It is over almost as suddenly as it began: the Burning Kor breaks apart, and the breach closes behind it. Azer saved, resentment of New Gravehold.
Its hoard holds treasures of legend: Celestial Slayer, Phantasmal Echo and Pyroclastic Slag. Among the ruins you find a survivor: Indira. A one-breach youngling lost in her Ordeal, found by Yan Magda — and denied mage rank by Brama for it Gravehold's chronicler of the War Eternal sieges.
Weeks later, the end of the old account still comes back to you: “They clear large tracts of land for their farms, and send out hunting parties, build distant outposts, and spread themselves carelessly across the surface. It is disrupting the natural patterns of the Portalborn. Their patrols and their walls drive them further south, into our forests, for sustenance.”
Then a runner finds your camp, with news of people frozen in green horror outside the walls.

**If you lose.**

Your breaches gutter out one by one, and there is nothing left to do but run. The Burning Kor lets you go.
You make a cold camp at the road out of Erythiza, and no one complains. Next time it will be ready for you. So will you.

## Battle 4 — Path B: A Market Turned Slaughterhouse

You march through a cold drizzle that does not stop for a day and a night.
The path ends at a market turned slaughterhouse. Stalls lie overturned, their awnings torn. No one has come back for the goods, or for the dead.
Something has passed this way. The signs are clear: icy trails descending into the dark; breath freezing. Follow the frost into the blackest black.
The bells are far away. There is no one to call for help.

### Absorbing Wraith

The ground shudders. Out of the dark, the Absorbing Wraith rises to meet you. Before you stands the cold hunter itself. It should not be here at all: it belongs to another age.
Quilius left a record of it: “Cold, colder, coldest. I see my breath in these caverns. The skin of my hand sticks to the glittering stones. The ragged torchlight gives light, but no warmth. My breath is warm, a piece of my own heat escaping from my teeth. Whatever is in these caverns has no heat. Its breath comes, frozen.”
You are not alone: Joanna is with you. But neither is the enemy — the Swarm has followed you here. Fight.

> **Friend / foe:** Joanna, the Merchant / The Swarm  
> **Treasures:** level 1: each player; level 2: one for the group; level 3: each player  
> **Nemesis deck (random basics from your collection):** T1: 3 nemesis + 2 basic + 3 upgraded basic · T2: 3 nemesis + 1 basic + 5 upgraded basic · T3: 3 nemesis + 0 basic + 7 upgraded basic  
> **Rewards:** level 4 treasures: Brilliant Sapphire, Planar Visions, Untethered Helix, Astral Tesseract, Quantum Transmogrifier; mage: Claudia (Legacy of Gravehold); player cards: Voidium Spike, Void Mill, Embody Flame, Vigorous Sunstone, Primordial Fetish, Descent of Destruction

**If you win.**

It is over almost as suddenly as it began: the Absorbing Wraith breaks apart, and the breach closes behind it. A step on Quilius' path.
Its hoard holds treasures of legend: Brilliant Sapphire, Planar Visions and Untethered Helix. Among the ruins you find a survivor: Claudia. Hyperactive genius daughter of Kadir and Malastar who feels everyone's aether; could not have an Ordeal because Gravehold was gone.
Weeks later, the end of the old account still comes back to you: “We've tracked the beast by the icy trails that descend into the dark. Alcheia lights the way in burning rubies, a point of life amongst the silent tomb. At night we eat the snow until our throats are numb, my fingers red and burning. Cracked skin. Blue lips. The silence beckons me closer, and I know my quarry rests in blackest black. And so too lies my destiny.”
Then a runner finds your camp, with news of people frozen in green horror outside the walls.

**If you lose.**

Your breaches gutter out one by one, and there is nothing left to do but run. The Absorbing Wraith lets you go.
You make a cold camp at a market turned slaughterhouse, and no one complains. Next time it will be ready for you. So will you.

## Battle 5: Green-glass Statues on a Hillside

There will be no more roads after this one, and everyone knows it.
Your road ends, for now, at green-glass statues on a hillside. Men and women stand frozen in green crystal, mouths open, hands raised. Some of them were kneeling.
Everywhere there are marks of it: people frozen in green horror outside the walls; tainted jade that calls the weak. Green crystal is creeping across the cave walls again.
The silence is the worst part.

### Blight Lord

The light dies around you. When it returns, the Blight Lord is already there. Now you see what the signs were warning you of: the crystallizer.
The chronicles of Gravehold record it: “The Blight Lord was the first of The Nameless to touch The World That Was. With a quick and deliberate hand, the great city of Karek was turned to a mountain of green glass, its many thousands of denizens entombed within. The harbor towns of M'hlark were next to fall, the warm waters lapping against the jagged, frozen shapes of those caught unaware.”
An old saying comes to mind: “Just outside the city walls there are men and women, frozen in green horror, like monuments to the Blight Lord's coming.” With Fawn beside you, the odds seem less impossible. Then the Anomaly shows itself. There is no more time. Open your breaches.

> **Friend / foe:** Fawn, the Alchemist / The Anomaly  
> **Treasures:** level 1: each player; level 2: one for the group; level 3: each player; level 4: each player  
> **Expedition modifier:** During setup, swap one of the nemesis turn order cards for the Unleash turn order card.  
> **Expedition modifier:** The Unleash from this card resolves after checking to see if the Tainted Track advances.  
> **Expedition modifier:** SETUP: Gravehold suffers 5 damage. Two different players each suffer 2 damage.  
> **Nemesis deck (random basics from your collection):** T1: 3 nemesis + 2 basic + 3 upgraded basic · T2: 3 nemesis + 1 basic + 5 upgraded basic · T3: 3 nemesis + 0 basic + 7 upgraded basic

**If you win.**

The breach shudders and folds in on itself. When the light returns, the Blight Lord is gone. Breach glass that paves the dirt market; a pouch of noble gems lost beneath the walls.
When the fighting is over, the rest of the old account comes back to you: “Even the very earth below its shadow became glass, left to shatter into crude and useless shapes. The elder of the First Tribe bade his people to pay tribute, for in his ignorance he considered it a divine messenger from the gods they worshiped then. Of those that knelt before it, none were spared. And thus the Blight Lord was given its name, for the certain ruin of our people followed it like some merciless plague.”
It is over. You stand together among the ruins of the battle, too tired to cheer, too alive to weep.

**If you lose.**

Blackness strobes at the edge of your vision — then Xaxos is standing over you, and the breach is fading. The Blight Lord has withdrawn, wounded but alive.
You find shelter at green-glass statues on a hillside and take stock: bruises, burns, and a little less pride. Out there in the dark, it is waiting.

### Epilogue

The road home is long, and you walk most of it in silence. When the walls finally come into view, Nook, Razra, Leisan and Taqren are still with you — changed, but alive.
You report to Raven, who listens to every word and thanks you — but before you leave, you learn that someone saw the insignia of Xaxos on a fleeing robe.
In the market, someone is already selling charms said to be blessed by your expedition. That night, the bells ring again.
