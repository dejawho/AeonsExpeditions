## Timeline

### 0. The Dawn of Breach Magic
*Thousands of years before the descent into Gravehold* — boxes: Origins, The Depths

Before humanity, the troggs (three tribes: Yud, Orp and Zom) shaped the world and then went to sleep in the cave that would become Gravehold. Much later, magic users called witches existed, but breach magic as we know it was born when Kiri Maesan, a young scientist from Erythiza, lived among the phoenixes in the jungle and learned to mimic the way they rip breaches between aether particles. Corruption already existed: animals that turned were culled by the phoenixes as part of a natural cycle.

- **The troggs shape the world** — The three trogg tribes — Yud (life, creation), Orp (shadow, death) and Zom (dreamers) — shape the young world in harmony, build Gravehold as a sanctuary from the chaos, then sleep in the deep cave, forgotten.
- **Kiri Maesan learns from the phoenixes** — A scientist from Erythiza follows a phoenix flock in the jungle for months, befriends a juvenile phoenix and copies its dive to open the first human breach. She abandons the word 'witch' and calls herself a breach mage. Her pyroclasm is later preserved in Karek's archive.

### 1. The World That Was
*Centuries before the descent* — boxes: Past and Future

A world of great sprawling cities: Erythiza (seat of the Royal Erythiza Academy and its university), Karek (a shining brass port city that held the First Tribe's great archive), Azrius, the harbor towns of M'hlark and Port Hanon with its City Guard. There were steam trains, inventors and scholars (Antoni Lexive, Kwananya Ja'ar, Kavoc, Auren). Corruption was a slow, inevitable fact of life: people who felt it rising would give up their names and descend to Gravehold, away from society — the origin of the word 'Nameless'. The world's true name, later recovered by Yan Magda, was Khasad Vol.

- **The age of scholars and inventors** — Antoni Lexive describes aether's communication properties, Kwananya Ja'ar studies aether nanoparticles, Kavoc probes 'the empty white space beyond our breaches' and leaves a compendium of thousands of spells. Auren sketches flying craft and mechanical prosthetics, blueprints that will be found centuries later by Rhys.
- **Giving up one's name** — Corruption creeps through everyone: tendrils, scales, fangs. The dignified descend to Gravehold before losing their minds; Port Hanon's guard beat back those who wait too long. Port Hanon itself is built on a Nameless burial ground.

### 2. The Great Calamity
*The fall of The World That Was* — boxes: Past and Future, Beyond the Breach, The Nameless, The Descent, Aeon's End (Core Box)

At the Royal Erythiza Academy, Dr. Soleil D'Arca studied The Hunger, a filter-feeder that metabolises corruption. When it fused with a boy it became the Oracle of Filth, strong enough to turn a healthy human into a Nameless in an instant. Erythiza vanished in a single day: an invisible eruption, a tsunami of corruption that turned citizens, plants, animals and even stones into monsters. The Blight Lord — the 'Blighted God' — eclipsed the moon over Karek and froze the kneeling First Tribe in green glass; the archivist Raven alone survived, hidden while giving birth to Xaxos. The sky became a starless abyss and the first Purge erased most of history.

- **The Hunger and the Oracle of Filth** — Dr. Soleil D'Arca's specimen, The Hunger, fuses with a boy in the Academy laboratory and becomes the Oracle of Filth, amplifying corruption exponentially.
- **Erythiza is consumed** — The great city vanishes in a day. A wave of corruption spreads outward; a market becomes a slaughter. Unknown heroes contain the source somewhere — young Brama wonders where they put it.
- **The night of emeralds** — The Blight Lord, first Nameless to touch the world, turns Karek and the harbor towns of M'hlark to green glass. The First Tribe's elder kneels to it as a divine messenger; none are spared except the archivist Raven and her newborn, Xaxos.

### 3. The Descent
*A generation after the Calamity* — boxes: The Descent, The Abyss, The Caverns

Survivors roam the wasteland in clans led by the Sisterhood: elders and sisters who speak the sister-words, followed by the 'Followers' (men and anyone who cannot learn the words), who hunt, heal, forge and carry. Young Brama, a Sister Initiate, believes a defensible city must still exist and leads a ragtag band on a pilgrimage — Janti, Thraxir, Leisan, Mazahaedron, Raven and the child Xaxos, Adelheim, Dalana, Fawn, Myrna, Joanna. They pass the dead town of Donnyton (The Coven), the swamp of The Infecter and the Blighted God's crystal fields, and finally find Gravehold, an abandoned underground citadel guarded by The Wrath and its last mad keeper. The conclaves split: half wanted to stay on the surface; Brama, with Thraxir at her side, led the rest underground and counselled collapsing the cave mouth.

- **Brama's pilgrimage** — Brama, a young sister, defies elders who call permanence hubris and leads her clan to look for a defensible city. Janti, a trans woman who earned the sister-words, and Thraxir, a Follower who opened a breach with his sword, are proof that the old rules are wrong.
- **Journey's End: finding Gravehold** — Under a red sun, The Wrath coils over the abandoned citadel. A lone corrupted keeper haunts its halls. Brama's people claim it; the cave mouth is collapsed to entomb them in safety.

### 4. Old Gravehold — The Long Siege
*Generations ('aeons') underground* — boxes: Aeon's End (Core Box), The Depths, The Nameless, War Eternal, The Outer Dark, The Void, The Returned, Tales of Old Gravehold, Beyond the Breach, The Caverns

Gravehold lives in darkness: the dirt market, the hovels, Chasmwalk, the balustrade, the crypts, The Depths where the dead are thrown. Breach mages turn the Nameless' own breaches into weapons. Brama, high elder, guards tradition and faith; Malastar trains younglings through the Ordeal; Thraxir, breach commander, dies to Rageborne and the post is never filled again. Wave after wave of Nameless besiege the city (Carapace Queen, Crooked Mask, Prince of Gluttons, Horde-Crone, Blight Lord's return, Wayward One...). Then exiles emerge from the Void — Yan Magda, freed from the Black Mausoleum, with her 'Void-tossed orphans' Garu, Dezmodia, Kain and Remnant — and fight beside Gravehold against the Thrice-Dead Prophet, from whose corpse the Umbra Titan bursts. Mist enters the breach a fourth time and returns as a 'vessel'. Xaxos founds a masked blood-magic circle in the Cragbellows, while the Knight of Shackles (the corrupted Thraxir) leads the dead against the city.

- **The first sieges** — Nameless come through breaches into the cave itself. The Prince of Gluttons, fourth to besiege the city, devours its memories — even the name of the world. Breach mages learn to use the breaches as weapons.
- **Yan Magda returns** — Sentenced by The Conclave to eternal sleep in the Black Mausoleum, Yan Magda is pulled out by Garu, wanders the spiral of worlds gathering Void orphans (Dezmodia, Garu, Kain, Remnant) and emerges in the caves, where she finds the child Indira lost in her Ordeal. She chooses friendship over vengeance.
- **Death of Thraxir** — Breach commander Thraxir is split in two by Rageborne. He is thrown into The Depths with the other dead; no one is ever elected to his post.
- **The Thrice-Dead Prophet** — Killed three times, the Prophet crushes Frayla and breaks the South Wall; Sparrow beheads it, and the Umbra Titan bursts from its neck. The Titan's shadow touches the coward Ohat and creates Ulgimor, who drives the Titan into The Depths.
- **Mist becomes the vessel** — On her fourth trip into the breach, a crystalline 'sound' enters Mist and remakes her. Gex calls her 'the being we once called Mist'.
- **The masked circle of the Cragbellows** — Xaxos and like-minded mages meet in robes and masks in the ruins of the Cragbellows, practising blood magic and listening to whispers from the breach. The Hollow Crown's acolytes were mages who practised forbidden rituals in the same rubble.

### 5. The Fall
*The end of Old Gravehold* — boxes: Legacy

Xaxos' experiment draws Maelstrom, a colossal orb of blades, through the largest breach ever seen. Gravehold burns. Dezmodia sacrifices herself, erupting in light and bringing the cavern down on Maelstrom. Survivors flee through the tunnels; Soren, Z'hana's husband, dies holding the line; Cairna is crushed and left behind; Ohat and Ulgimor survive alone in the ruins for decades. Xaxos flees into the Void. Mist finds that the surface, believed dead, is green and alive.

- **Maelstrom** — Xaxos' experiment pulls Maelstrom through. Gravehold burns; Dezmodia erupts and buries it under the collapsing cavern. Xaxos vanishes into the Void ('I'm sorry...').
- **The surface is alive** — Tracking Bladius' minions upward, Mist finds forests, streams and sky. The survivors found a camp that becomes New Gravehold.

### 6. New Gravehold
*The decades after The Fall* — boxes: Legacy, Buried Secrets, Into The Wild, Shattered Dreams, The Ancients, The New Age, Outcasts, Return To Gravehold, Southern Village

Humanity rebuilds on the surface: farms, outposts (Southpost, the Northern Outpost), walls twice as high. The Legacy generation of mages defeats Spawning Horror, Fungal Mesh, Haze Fiend, Deathmind, Maelstrom: Risen and finally Xaxos: Ascended. Brama exiles Xaxos' students; Arachnos rebels, is banished to the woods and experiments on Nameless (Maggoth, Fenrix, Ageless Walker, Experiment 153). New mages appear: Inco the plant-raised boy, Razra and her beast Rip, Mazra the prodigy, Talix of Southpost, Soskel, Rhia, Claudia, Gygar, Sahala, Qu. A traitor mage uses glyphs to drive Necroswarm against Southpost — it is Z'hana. Mages discover Azer, an older surface village of Tethered mages, and dive into sunken Old Gravehold. A redeemed Xaxos returns from the Void and leads an expedition to the Fountain of Souls, where Dezmodia and Arachnos are reborn and Thraxir regains his mind.

- **The Legacy campaign** — New mages defeat Spawning Horror (its pods become equipment), free Brama from Fungal Mesh, survive Haze Fiend, protect their memories from Deathmind, and bring down Maelstrom: Risen. Xaxos, 'having drunk from the well from which the Nameless draw power', is defeated — dragged into the Void or fleeing broken.
- **Arachnos' rebellion and exile** — Arachnos continues Xaxos' research, is condemned by the Council, leads a rebellion and is banished with his followers to the woods, where his rituals create monsters and eventually fuse him with a Void spider.
- **The New Age expedition** — Commander Soskel's northern expedition vanishes with rare artifacts. A rescue team meets Rhia, frees Soskel from Arachnos, saves Talix from the Ageless Walker, defeats Fenrix in a summer blizzard and finds Lost, an amnesiac mage bound in fiery armour, in a hidden bunker.
- **Into The Wild: the traitor mage** — Inco feels the plants die. Razra and Rip, seeking the cause, are captured by a cloaked woman using glyphs that lure Nameless and drain human aether. Necroswarm — Inco's childhood caretaker — is driven against Southpost. The traitor bears Xaxos' insignia and a veteran's ankle tattoo. The Council hides the truth.
- **The Wailing and The Wanderer** — Blue onyx spreads through the forest from a screaming flying Nameless. Brama and Mazra find in the archives that The Wanderer is related to the Wayward One.
- **Return to sunken Gravehold** — Gygar's water-breathing expedition finds the Fortress — a Nameless shelled in the rubble of their homes — the Burrower, Cairna living alone on legs of stone, and Ohat with Ulgimor.
- **The Burning Kor reaches Azer** — New Gravehold's farms and patrols push Portalborn south; a man of flame burns Azer's forests. Lucien, who lost his Tether, and Reth, who never had one, stand against it.
- **Invasion of the Void** — Xaxos, redeemed, returns to ask for help: the Fountain of Souls, a living pool in the Void, swallows the aether that crosses breaches. Mages fight inside the Void protecting Xaxos: Outcast; Dezmodia and Arachnos are reborn and Thraxir regains his mind.

### 7. The Civil War and the Second Fall
*About a generation after The Fall* — boxes: Legacy of Gravehold, The Ruins, The Surface

Wild Void portals open everywhere. Brama's paranoia hardens — Yan Magda, Garu, Mist and the 'Void mages' are exiled — while Z'hana, allied with Azer, demands freedom to study the Void. The Bishop of Scrolls narrates it all as a fairy tale and gathers an army of Nameless in the Void. The war ends with Mist revealed as the architect of a 'Second Fall', a manufactured apocalypse meant to unite humanity against her and birth 'the World To Come'. The outcome branches: New Gravehold is forever changed.

- **The exiles** — Brama exiles Arachnos and Karak, forbids Mist from Voidwalking, exiles Garu for opening a Void portal and finally sentences Yan Magda to a cottage in the wilds. Indira watches and hates herself for it.
- **Brama against Z'hana** — Z'hana, unmasked as the traitor and allied with Azer, claims a city must be a haven for everyone, dangerous or not. Families and mentors pick sides: Malastar and Kadir with tradition, Taqren torn, Razra ready to forgive after one punch.
- **Mist Revealed** — Mist raises her arms to the Void: a Second Fall, to make humanity hate her as one and build 'the World To Come'.
- **Inco's pilgrimage** — After the war, Inco carries a sapling grown where Necroswarm fell into the Void and plants it in a blooming swamp grove, where strange glassy-eyed children play.

### 8. The Scattered Age
*Decades to centuries after New Gravehold* — boxes: Evolution, Past and Future, Beyond the Breach, System Overload, The Cinderkeep, The Wastes

Dorian bends causality decades on. Hundreds of years later, humanity lives in small villages around old ruins: Souston, protected by head woman Camellia's glyph-carving; Faen in the eastern swamp, whose Tethers were once turned against each other; the line of Xaxos still bearing fangs and claws (Naftir); Lilly — by every clue the daughter of Razra and Inco — lost in the Void where it 'isn't sundown yet'. Further still rises Cindercrown, a city of iron walls ruled by the Regi, built over 'the System' — sealed tunnels where Nameless are buried — patrolled by Strike teams, the heirs of the old dagger captains. Through time-travel experiments (Nook's Anomaly), Cindercrown's era loops back into Old Gravehold's.

- **Villages around the ruins** — Souston, Faen and other villages live in peace thanks to glyph-carving; heroes like Bayli long for the legends of Old Gravehold. Xaxos' descendants still carry the Void in their bodies.
- **Cindercrown and the System** — Strike teams descend into the System beneath the iron city of Cindercrown, where the Terror in Brass swims through metal. Professor Stake goes looking for relics; Nook's time experiments threaten to snap time itself.

## Places

In The World That Was the corrupted 'descended to Gravehold' when they gave up their names. Brama's people claimed it after the Calamity and lived there for generations until The Fall; its ruins later flooded.
- **The Depths** (underground) — The infinite tunnels and vaults below Gravehold: where the troggs slept, where the dead are thrown because dirt is too precious to bury them, where husk carcasses are heaped, where the Ordeal tests young mages and where Kadir seeks a sleeping god and a shadow city older than time.
- **The Cragbellows / Craghollows** (ruins) — Ruined district of Gravehold where masked mages practised blood magic — Xaxos' secret circle and the Hollow Crown's acolytes.

## Factions

- **The Sisterhood (conclaves of sisters)** — Nomadic clans led by elders and sisters who speak the sister-words; men and non-speakers are 'Followers'. Its elders consider permanence hubris. The conclaves split over going underground.
- **The Followers** — Hunters, fathers, alchemists and healers who keep the Sisterhood fed but are belittled by it.
- **The First Tribe** — Archivists of Karek who would not abandon their knowledge; frozen in green glass when they knelt to the Blight Lord. Raven and Xaxos are its last.
- **The Breach Mages of Gravehold** — Defenders who weaponise breaches. Ranks: youngling, initiate, apprentice, mage, elder, high elder; offices of dagger captain and breach commander. Veterans bear an ankle tattoo.
- **Council of Elders** — Senior mages governing Gravehold and New Gravehold, led by Brama. Debates endlessly; hides inconvenient truths 'to avoid chaos'.
- **Followers of Xaxos** — Students and sympathisers of Xaxos' Void research, exiled to the woods. They wear his insignia, release captured Nameless, and some become Nameless themselves.
- **Acolytes of the Hollow Crown** — Former mages who practised blood magic in the Craghollows and now serve the Crown masked: Edryss Tragg, Holadran, Kurgax, Lurzan, Mord, Nhavkalas, Ren-Goda, Solara, Bezu.
- **Yan Magda's cadre of Void orphans** — Exiles gathered in the spiral of worlds: Garu, Dezmodia, Kain, Remnant. Distrusted as heretics, they save Gravehold against the Prophet.
- **The Conclave** — An ancient order that does not believe in killing; it sentenced Yan Magda to eternal sleep. It worshipped at a great tower and reveres a child 'risen from the dust', the first of their kind.
- **The Monks of the Caverns** — Ascetics teaching five steps to enlightenment through the kill.
- **Henge Mystics** — Mystics who hear the world as a living mother; Mazahaedron never lets his feet touch the earth.
- **Mages of Azer (Tethers)** — Paired mages (source and sink) bound by tattoos; they ally with Z'hana in the civil war.
- **Royal Erythiza Academy** — Scientific institution whose head scientist's experiment with The Hunger triggered the Calamity.
- **Port Hanon City Guard** — Guards who beat back the feral corrupted.
- **Strike teams of Cindercrown** — Captain-and-scout teams that patrol the System, successors of the dagger captains; equipped by the Allocators from the treasury.
- **The Bishop's army** — Nameless collected across the Void by the Bishop of Scrolls, some pulled half-formed from the Fountain of Souls.

## Concepts

- **Aether** — The building block of life and magic, present in everything (the Azerian view) and concentrated in gems; the aether spilled by the Nameless became trapped in stones.
- **Void essence** — Xaxos' term for aether's opposite: concentrated entropy that breaks things down and turns living things into twisted reflections of themselves. Leaks through breaches; marks bodies with scales, claws, tendrils.
- **Breaches** — Tears in reality opened by rotating aether to 'dimensional velocity' — first learned from phoenixes. The Nameless travel through them; mages channel spells through up to four of their own.
- **The sister-words** — The mage's tongue, 'the language of all things', taught by Brama and once reserved to the Sisterhood. Exceptions prove the rule wrong: Janti, Thraxir, Xaxos' runes, Cairna's instinctive magic.
- **The Nameless (Portalborn, Voidlife)** — World-culling beings from the Void. Clues across eras: in the past, corrupted humans gave up their names; the Fountain of Souls reconstitutes the aether of fallen mages into Nameless; partly corrupted humans (Arachnos, the Bishop, Crooked Mask) can still speak. 'The Nameless do not die, for they themselves are death.'
- **Corruption** — The slow transformation of living things toward Nameless; inheritable (Xaxos' line), accelerated by Void exposure, culled in nature by phoenixes.
- **The Ordeal** — Mage initiation: learn the sister-words, fashion a personal gem, find your way back alone from the deep with Kadir's guidance, then slay a snared Void beast dropped down a shaft outside Chasmwalk.
- **Tethers** — Azerian practice of pairing mages as source and sink, marked with matching tattoos; love and weakness at once.
- **Glyphs, sigils and runes** — Aether drawn in patterns to steer small Void creatures (Azer, Souston) — or, with human aether, to lure and control great Nameless (Z'hana).
- **The Fall** — Name given (by Xaxos) to Gravehold's destruction by Maelstrom.
- **The Second Fall / The World To Come** — Mist's planned apocalypse to unite humanity.
- **Khasad Vol** — The forgotten true name of The World That Was, spoken aloud by Yan Magda.
- **The Fountain of Souls** — A living pool in the Void that absorbs aether crossing breaches; bubbles inside it are almost recognisable — souls.
- **Pulse magic** — Electric energy that began running through mages after the Legacy era, converted into charges.

## Characters

### Brama — *Breach Mage Elder, Sister Initiate, the Leader*
The oldest living human, a young Sister Initiate during the Calamity who defied her elders to lead a pilgrimage to a defensible city and found Gravehold. She brought the sister-words down from The World That Was, counselled sealing the cave and became high elder. Her faith is Gravehold's best weapon — and with age it hardens into paranoia: she exiles Yan Magda, Garu, Arachnos and Xaxos' students, and in the civil war becomes a nemesis to Z'hana's side. Once the rebel against tradition, she becomes the institution.

Eras: The Great Calamity, The Descent, Old Gravehold — The Long Siege, The Fall, New Gravehold, The Civil War and the Second Fall. Versions: [Brama (The Descent)](https://aeonsend.wiki.gg/wiki/Brama_(The_Descent)), [Brama](https://aeonsend.wiki.gg/wiki/Brama), [Brama (Legacy of Gravehold)](https://aeonsend.wiki.gg/wiki/Brama_(Legacy_of_Gravehold))

### Xaxos — *Breach Mage Adept, Voidbringer, Ascended, Outcast, Reckoner, the Arcane Researcher*
Born in Karek's archive as the First Tribe froze, raised by the archivist Raven (who called the child her 'little girl') and then by Brama. Unable to learn the sister-words like the other girls, Xaxos studied the tribe's books, runes and the Void. As a Gravehold adept he preached learning from the Nameless, led a masked blood-magic circle and, seeking power to protect the city, drew Maelstrom into Gravehold — causing The Fall and Dezmodia's death. He fled into the Void, returned 'Ascended' and was defeated; in exile he coined 'Void essence', rescued a girl from the Changeling Nest and raised her as his daughter Qu, fought the Reliquary and came back redeemed as the Outcast to face the Fountain of Souls. His descendants still carry claws and fangs.

Eras: The Great Calamity, The Descent, Old Gravehold — The Long Siege, The Fall, New Gravehold, The Civil War and the Second Fall. Versions: [Xaxos, the Arcane Researcher](https://aeonsend.wiki.gg/wiki/Xaxos,_the_Arcane_Researcher), [Xaxos (The Abyss)](https://aeonsend.wiki.gg/wiki/Xaxos_(The_Abyss)), [Xaxos](https://aeonsend.wiki.gg/wiki/Xaxos), [Xaxos (The Void)](https://aeonsend.wiki.gg/wiki/Xaxos_(The_Void)), [Xaxos: Ascended](https://aeonsend.wiki.gg/wiki/Xaxos:_Ascended), [Xaxos: Outcast](https://aeonsend.wiki.gg/wiki/Xaxos:_Outcast), [Xaxos (Legacy of Gravehold)](https://aeonsend.wiki.gg/wiki/Xaxos_(Legacy_of_Gravehold))

### Mist — *Breach Mage Dagger Captain, Voidwalker*
Dagger captain who rose through the guard 'as though born for the task' and walked into the breach three times; not brave, only desperate to live. On the fourth trip a crystalline sound entered and remade her: 'I am the vessel now.' She saves survivors at The Fall, discovers the living surface, teaches adult initiates like Soskel, and is forbidden to Voidwalk. In the end she becomes the final villain, Mist Revealed, unleashing a Second Fall to unite humanity against her.

Eras: Old Gravehold — The Long Siege, The Fall, New Gravehold, The Civil War and the Second Fall. Versions: [Mist](https://aeonsend.wiki.gg/wiki/Mist), [Mist (War Eternal)](https://aeonsend.wiki.gg/wiki/Mist_(War_Eternal)), [Mist Revealed](https://aeonsend.wiki.gg/wiki/Mist_Revealed)

### Malastar — *Breach Mage Mentor, inventor*
Trainer of younglings and designer of the Ordeal; later New Gravehold's forge-master who crafts equipment from Nameless remains and invents synthetic gems. Married to Kadir, father of Claudia; thinks of Z'hana as a sister but sides against her.

Eras: Old Gravehold — The Long Siege, The Fall, New Gravehold, The Civil War and the Second Fall. Versions: [Malastar](https://aeonsend.wiki.gg/wiki/Malastar), [Malastar (Legacy of Gravehold)](https://aeonsend.wiki.gg/wiki/Malastar_(Legacy_of_Gravehold))

### Kadir — *Breach Mage Delver, scientist*
Delver of the deeps who seeks a sleeping god and a shadow city beneath Gravehold, and guides younglings on their Ordeal. Later a gem scientist on the surface, married to Malastar, mother of Claudia; a staunch traditionalist against Z'hana.

Eras: Old Gravehold — The Long Siege, New Gravehold, The Civil War and the Second Fall. Versions: [Kadir](https://aeonsend.wiki.gg/wiki/Kadir), [Kadir (Legacy of Gravehold)](https://aeonsend.wiki.gg/wiki/Kadir_(Legacy_of_Gravehold))

### Adelheim — *Breach Mage Weaponsmith, the Blacksmith*
Gruff, loyal smith who mends looted, blood-rusted weapons on the pilgrimage and later forges breach-ore blades and odd contraptions in Gravehold; admires the craftsmanship of monsters.

Eras: The Descent, Old Gravehold — The Long Siege. Versions: [Adelheim, the Blacksmith](https://aeonsend.wiki.gg/wiki/Adelheim,_the_Blacksmith), [Adelheim](https://aeonsend.wiki.gg/wiki/Adelheim)

### Jian — *Breach Mage Orphan*

## Nemeses

- **[Rageborne](https://aeonsend.wiki.gg/wiki/Rageborne)** (champion / executioner, Old Gravehold — The Long Siege) — The Nameless' champion, sent once Gravehold became a worthy foe; it killed breach commander Thraxir. *Hook:* The executioner of Gravehold returns to finish what it started.
- **[Carapace Queen](https://aeonsend.wiki.gg/wiki/Carapace_Queen)** (horde mother, Old Gravehold — The Long Siege) — Mother-of-Husks whose clockwork-like swarms have brought the most ruin upon Gravehold; destroyed before The Fall, but a Broodwomb survived. *Hook:* 'To kill one is to breed six' — the husks are coming again.
- **[Crooked Mask](https://aeonsend.wiki.gg/wiki/Crooked_Mask)** (corrupting god, Old Gravehold — The Long Siege) — The god of corruption through temptation; its laugh is heard under the bells. Its Corrupters spread madness through the weak. *Hook:* Something is offering Gravehold's hungry exactly what they need.
- **[Crooked Mask (Beyond the Breach)](https://aeonsend.wiki.gg/wiki/Crooked_Mask_(Beyond_the_Breach))** (corrupted human, Old Gravehold — The Long Siege) — Indira realises the Mask laughs because it was once human — a mage corrupted enough to go mad but not wholly monster. *Hook:* Somewhere close, a laugh rings out — and it sounds almost familiar.
- **[Prince of Gluttons](https://aeonsend.wiki.gg/wiki/Prince_of_Gluttons)** (memory eater, Old Gravehold — The Long Siege) — Fourth Nameless to besiege Gravehold; it devoured the city's history, even the name of the world, leaving only fear. *Hook:* The archive's records are vanishing word by word.
- **[Horde-Crone](https://aeonsend.wiki.gg/wiki/Horde-Crone)** (awakener of the ancients, Old Gravehold — The Long Siege) — Stirred the trogg tribes (Yud, Orp, Zom) from their slumber in the deep to reclaim the city they built. *Hook:* Gravehold's first builders want their home back — and they may have the right.
- **[Blight Lord](https://aeonsend.wiki.gg/wiki/Blight_Lord)** (crystallizer, The Great Calamity) — First Nameless to touch The World That Was; turned Karek and M'hlark to green glass. *Hook:* Green crystal is creeping across the cave walls again.
- **[The Blight](https://aeonsend.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Blight)** (force of nature, The Great Calamity) — The Blighted God in the age of the pilgrimage, guarding its crystal fields full of Raven's petrified family. *Hook:* The only road to safety passes through a field of crystal statues.
- **[Wayward One](https://aeonsend.wiki.gg/wiki/Wayward_One)** (lost ghost, Old Gravehold — The Long Siege) — Trapped neither here nor there, wailing; touching its dust fades you away. The mages fight it out of compassion. *Hook:* A tormented Nameless seeks an end to its struggle — give it one.
- **[The Wanderer](https://aeonsend.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Wanderer)** (lost ghost (relative), New Gravehold) — Kin of another wandering Nameless, identified in the archives by Brama and Mazra. *Hook:* Scouts describe something Brama has fought before — the archives must say what.
- **[Wayward Reflection](https://aeonsend.wiki.gg/wiki/Wayward_Reflection)** (dimensional echo, The Scattered Age) — A creature in two places at once, trapped in the borderland of a breach. *Hook:* Two sightings, miles apart, of the same Nameless at the same moment.
- **[Gate Witch](https://aeonsend.wiki.gg/wiki/Gate_Witch)** (mistress of time, Old Gravehold — The Long Siege) — The 'ragged face of the infinite' Yan Magda saw in the Void; bends and breaks time. *Hook:* Time is running out — literally.
- **[Hollow Crown](https://aeonsend.wiki.gg/wiki/Hollow_Crown)** (false god, Old Gravehold — The Long Siege) — Compels worship rather than fear; its masked acolytes were mages practising blood magic in the Craghollows. *Hook:* Missing mages are seen kneeling in the ruins, masked.
- **[Magus of Cloaks](https://aeonsend.wiki.gg/wiki/Magus_of_Cloaks)** (shadow lord, Old Gravehold — The Long Siege) — A thing of darkness: every blow peels away one cloak of shadow to reveal another. *Hook:* The torches of Gravehold go out one by one.

## Hidden texts

- **The Anomaly confession (Legacy of Gravehold)** (Flavor texts of the five Anomaly cards read in order Nihilum → Eternus → Photonix → Zeronia → Mechanek.): “...that I saw something in the Void when I experimented with it. There was a feeling that the very dimension itself was looking back through my breaches and seeing me, and trying to draw me further into it. I have never forgotten the way the air tasted, like a stolen breath while plummeting to my death. I still wake up in the middle of the night remembering”
- **The crystal and spark riddles (Beyond the Breach)** (Superior → Dim → Infused → Empty starter cards.): “When you spend enough time looking in one direction, you think if you turn around you'll be losing something; it becomes harder and harder to imagine going back, but progress is progress, no matter your facing. / If you look the same person in the eye for long enough, you begin to forget what the rest of their face looks like. It is not betrayal to take a step back every now and again, and take in the truth of all your surroundings.”
